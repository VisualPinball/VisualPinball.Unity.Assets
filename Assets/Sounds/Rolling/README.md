# Rolling audio presets

These assets pair a surface's acoustic material with a ball's acoustic material. Wood, Plastic Ramp and Wire Rails provide separate Steel and Ceramic responses. Tagged physics materials and support prefabs demonstrate inheritance even when local numerical physics coefficients are overridden. The support templates are invisible collider geometry intended to be resized/positioned by a table author; they do not replace an existing table's physics tuning.

The Steel loops were extracted from `Terminator 2 (Williams 1991) g5k 1.25.vpx`. Its twelve `BallRoll_*` files are byte-identical and share `BallRoll_0.wav`. All ten plastic and ten wire variants remain available; T2 uses a shared six-slot ramp pool. Stereo recordings are retained in `Sources~`, and playback WAVs contain their arithmetic channel mean without normalization or resampling. See `Sources~/provenance.json` for file and PCM hashes and the exact recipe. Existing mechanical and collision clips are unchanged.

The Ceramic loops and short material-seam samples are original offline modal synthesis, explicitly named `(Synthesized)` in the sound banks. They are distinct source PCM, not renamed or pitch-shifted steel loops, and are starting points for tables without measured ceramic recordings. They do not claim to reproduce a recorded Powerball. The generator and its provenance are in `Sources~`; the generated PCM is dedicated under CC0-1.0. T2's wire entry/exit recordings are also available as optional boundary banks. Source VPX recording provenance is retained; this does not change the original recordings' licensing terms.

T2's table gain is 0.8, with playfield/ramp gains of 0.5. The Steel profiles use integer XY speed, a threshold of 2, cubic gain coefficient 0.00011 and the original frequency-offset pitch equations. Disable the table's Material Transition Sounds when comparing strictly against the VPX script; its active rolling routines contain no boundary one-shots. Air boundary sounds are off by default to avoid duplicating collision/landing audio.

The `.vpe` exporter packages all reachable response rows and WAVs. `Sources~` is authoring provenance and is not a runtime dependency.
